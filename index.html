Each ingredient explained individually below.

Saw Palmetto

A well-known palm extract traditionally used in men's prostate and urinary health formulas; it is the ingredient most people associate with this category of supplement.

Pomegranate Extract

Supplies polyphenol antioxidants that help protect cells from oxidative stress and are commonly included in male vitality blends.

Zinc

A trace mineral involved in normal immune function, hormone metabolism and prostate tissue health; men's formulas frequently include it as a baseline nutrient.

Vitamin E

A fat-soluble antioxidant vitamin that helps shield cell membranes from oxidative damage and supports general cardiovascular and cellular wellness.

Selenium

A mineral that works with antioxidant enzymes in the body and is often paired with zinc and vitamin E in prostate-focused formulas.

Pygeum Africanum

An African tree bark extract long used in traditional men's urinary tract formulas and frequently combined with saw palmetto.

7

Nettle Root Extract

Derived from stinging nettle root, traditionally used to support normal urinary flow and comfort in men's health blends.

8

Lycopene

The red carotenoid found in tomatoes, valued as an antioxidant and commonly included in prostate wellness formulas.
